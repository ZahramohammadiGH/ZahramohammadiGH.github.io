Research
Research & Scientific Contributions

Research activities have been conducted in electrical engineering, with a particular emphasis on wireless communications, signal processing, statistical detection theory, mathematical optimization, and artificial intelligence. Research contributions include spectrum sensing in cognitive radio networks, lifetime optimization of underwater acoustic sensor networks, and the development of analytical and computational methods for complex communication-system problems. Following the completion of doctoral studies in Electrical Engineering at Shahid Bahonar University of Kerman, research has also been extended to multi-objective optimization and multi-robot path planning. Furthermore, AI-based and computational approaches to solving complex mathematical and engineering problems have been investigated, with an emphasis on the integration of theoretical methods and practical engineering applications.

June 2022 - Present

Industrial Automation
Industrial Automation & Electrical Engineering

Industrial automation, PLC programming, electrical troubleshooting, and maintenance activities have been carried out at Hezar Aluminum Factory. Practical experience has been acquired in the development and modification of industrial control programs using Siemens SIMATIC Manager, Siemens STEP 7 Micro/WIN, and FATEK WinProLadder. Active technical contributions have been made to the development of an automatic punching machine, particularly in PLC program development and Human–Machine Interface (HMI) design and implementation. These activities have involved the integration of electrical control systems, automation software, and operator interfaces to support reliable machine operation and improved production performance.

Sep 2018 - Present

Teaching
